Read the summary

Written by William Shakespeare in the 1590s, Romeo and Juliet is set in fair Verona, where two noble families have fought for generations. Romeo of the Montagues and Juliet of the Capulets fall in love at a masked feast and marry in secret. A duel, a banishment and a letter that never arrives tear them apart, and they die for a love that came minutes too late. Only then do their families make peace.
