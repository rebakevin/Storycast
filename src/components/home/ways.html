Three ways to take in a story

  1. 01

    Watch

    Play the video, with captions on if you like and the transcript following along beneath it.

  2. 02

    Listen

    Switch to audio only, for when your eyes are busy. The transcript is there if you want to read along.

  3. 03

    Read

    Prefer words? Read the summary, then follow the story chapter by chapter at your own pace.
