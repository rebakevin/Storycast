About & Access

StoryCast retells classic stories as short chapters, films and voices. Our mission is simple: nobody should be left outside the telling.

1. Accessibility features

1.1 Captions. Every video has English captions you can switch on or off.

1.2 Transcripts. Every video and audio piece has a full written transcript under it.

1.3 Keyboard. Every link, player and menu works with the keyboard, with a clear focus outline.

1.4 Contrast and size. Text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and scales with your browser zoom. Meaning never depends on colour alone.

1.5 Responsive. Pages adapt to phones, tablets and desktops, with no sideways scrolling and touch targets that are easy to tap.

1.6 Calm motion. Nothing autoplays. If your device asks for reduced motion, transitions are switched off.

2. How to engage

2.1 Read. Follow a story chapter by chapter at your own pace.

2.2 Watch. Start with the short film, with captions on if you like.

2.3 Listen. Close the story with the audio piece, or read along with the transcript.
