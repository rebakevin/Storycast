Romeo & Juliet

Two houses, one ancient grudge, and a love that could not wait for peace.

Begin the story ↓

The world of Verona

A city that cannot forget a quarrel.

Written by William Shakespeare in the 1590s, Romeo and Juliet is set in fair Verona, where two noble families have fought for generations. Their servants brawl in the streets; their children inherit the quarrel before they can understand it.

The Montagues

Charcoal line · Romeo

Romeo's house. Hot-blooded and loyal, they answer insult with steel.

The Capulets

Crimson line · Juliet

Juliet's house. Proud and wealthy, they guard their name above their daughter.

Meet the characters

  1. I

    Romeo Montague

    The lover

    A young, restless romantic, sick with love for a girl who does not love him — until he meets Juliet and forgets her entirely.

  2. II

    Juliet Capulet

    The daughter

    Not yet fourteen, sharp-minded and brave. She is the first to say what love will cost, and the first to pay it.

  3. III

    Mercutio

    The friend

    Romeo's quick-witted kinsman of the Prince. He mocks love, mocks fate, and is never far from a quarrel.

  4. IV

    Tybalt

    The rival

    Juliet's fiery cousin. For him the feud is a point of honour, and Romeo's presence at the feast is an insult.

  5. V

    Friar Laurence

    The confessor

    A gentle priest and herbalist who hopes a secret marriage might mend the city. His good intentions set the tragedy in motion.

Now, follow them out of the feast.

II · The Journey →

Chapter II · Four scenes

The Journey

Romeo in charcoal, Juliet in crimson. Watch how close they stand in each scene.

  1. i.

    The Capulet party

    Romeo slips into the Capulet feast in disguise, hoping only to glimpse Rosaline. Instead he meets Juliet. They speak in a shared sonnet, and kiss before either learns the other’s name.

  2. ii.

    The balcony & the secret marriage

    Beneath her window Romeo overhears Juliet wish away her name. They vow to love in spite of it. Next day Friar Laurence marries them in secret, hoping it will end the feud.

  3. iii.

    Mercutio & Tybalt fall

    Tybalt challenges Romeo, who refuses to fight his new kinsman. Mercutio fights instead and is killed. Blind with grief, Romeo kills Tybalt, and the feud claims a new debt.

  4. iv.

    Banishment & the desperate plan

    The Prince spares Romeo’s life but banishes him from Verona. With Juliet promised to another man, Friar Laurence offers a way out: a potion that will make her seem dead, and a letter to bring Romeo home.

A letter is sent. It does not arrive.

III · The Tragedy →

Chapter III · The last night

The Tragedy

Juliet's plan

Juliet drinks the Friar's potion and is laid in the Capulet tomb, cold as stone. But the messenger carrying Laurence's letter is stopped by plague quarantine. Romeo never reads it.

The final tragedy

Romeo hears only that Juliet is dead. He buys poison, breaks into the tomb, and drinks beside her. Moments later she wakes, finds him lifeless, and takes his dagger. Both die for a love that came minutes too late.

Reconciliation

At dawn, the Prince gathers both houses at the tomb. Montague and Capulet, finally face to face, see what their hatred has cost. They clasp hands and promise statues of gold for the children they lost.

What the story means

Love
It arrives at first sight and outruns every rule. Love gives Romeo and Juliet courage, and it gives them no time.
Fate
A plague, a missed letter, a minute’s delay. The play calls its lovers star-crossed, yet each small failure was human.
Hatred
An old grudge no one can explain. It is inherited, not chosen, and it poisons everyone it touches.
Consequences
Every choice, from a duel to a secret, adds weight. Peace comes only after the cost is counted in children.

Listen · 1 min

The Prince's last words

Read the transcript

A slow strings drone. The Prince speaks over the tomb: a morning of sorrow, peace bought with two lives, and a vow that Verona will remember them.
